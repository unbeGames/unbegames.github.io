### Changes:

- Base speed of Inverter signal decreased from 36 to 32. Level 016.00 and other similar will be much easier now.
- Increased amount of energy received on levels 024.04, 024.05, 024.08 and 048.00 to support more different strategies.
- Levels 024.27 and 024.29 was adjusted to deny "cheat" strategies in them.
- Improved layout of level 048.00. It became more forgivable to mistakes.

### Fixes:

- Fixed an issue, when timer node continue to work after you have lost.
- "Depth of Field" effect returned to world camera.
- Fixed missing tooltip on nuke upgrade.
- Fixed wrong button tooltip on "Capture" action.
- Fixed an issue when part of 013.00 level was not loaded.
- Fixed some issues in the final sequence.
