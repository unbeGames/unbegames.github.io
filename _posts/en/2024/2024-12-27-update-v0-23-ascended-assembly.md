Welcome, fellow rocket scientists and pilots, to Update v0.23 “Ascended Assembly”!

This time I brought you:

## Dynamic attachments

I've talked about this attachment system rework in depth in [my blog post](https://blog.unbegames.com/en/posts/2024/dev-update-assembly-rework-stage-3/). Long story short: you can finally place rocket parts radially almost whenever you want.

![](https://media1.giphy.com/media/v1.Y2lkPTc5MGI3NjExcGl0dml1aGEwOTZobGxjbXE4YTZjMnM4cmlveGE0ZDNpbW5kNXR2OCZlcD12MV9pbnRlcm5hbF9naWZfYnlfaWQmY3Q9Zw/AYqVvFqYulSYxHcYUW/giphy.gif)

The game will create radial attachments automatically for you. This will unlock the full potential of vessel crafting. Not only that, but there are more advantages to the new system: Dynamic attachments work great especially for planes, they can finally be properly balanced.

![](https://media4.giphy.com/media/v1.Y2lkPTc5MGI3NjExc2ViNzRhNGs5a29jeHI2NTdtMzlsb2x5cGV2cXljOHoxZDljMndpaSZlcD12MV9pbnRlcm5hbF9naWZfYnlfaWQmY3Q9Zw/rbUthuT1SM160gXat9/giphy.gif)

You will be able to attach most engines to the sides of fuel tanks. Some interesting rocket designs are incoming!

![](https://media1.giphy.com/media/v1.Y2lkPTc5MGI3NjExY2lud2Jtbm9tMGhncHhxbnIwOXRqcm03dDZocWJwcHdobmRpeXJ5NyZlcD12MV9pbnRlcm5hbF9naWZfYnlfaWQmY3Q9Zw/KerSbG2DVqKW3lhD6e/giphy.gif)

Cargo parts will be much more useful, as you'll be able to attach other parts anywhere inside it.

![](https://media3.giphy.com/media/v1.Y2lkPTc5MGI3NjExYzc3YnNzYWVmOHY4aXhnY2c2d2JheGl2NDc5MDVjMHVhZzN5NGVwYSZlcD12MV9pbnRlcm5hbF9naWZfYnlfaWQmY3Q9Zw/TlRaO3pGyuF9lm2MWA/giphy.gif)

Payload fairings now attach to the side of any cylindrical part of the appropriate radius. It should be much easier to use them now.

![](https://clan.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/images/34094219/144e13dc11e96fc1a06ea6e354731d345921b642.gif)

This system is a bit different than what you are used to so I strongly recommend finishing a newly added “**Dynamic attachments**” tutorial in the control center. That helps you understand how this system works and also teaches you some useful hotkeys. Also, it’s worth noting that a lot of rocket parts were reworked by changing size, shape or other parameters especially from the structure category. Now part radius and number of sockets are much more consistent.

## Multiple launch sites support

Another highly requested feature. Now you can launch spacecrafts from different locations. I’ve added the Baikonur cosmodrome as a feature test case. It consists only of a launchpad now, but I will expand it into a proper launch site in the future. Since the location of Baikonur is a bit superior to the main space center, it will be locked until you complete the “Land on the Moon” contract.

![](https://clan.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/images/34094219/ce8a10fbd968b724942662a27fbe842e0e34f045.png)
_Those players, who are following the development of the game since the begining, may recognize this launchpad_

More importantly, you can add launch facilities to the surface mods now. So I am looking forward to mod where I can launch my rocket from Kennedy Space Center…

There are a lot of other changes and improvements in the game. Like now you can control camera in the assembly shop using WASD or recover vessels that landed safely (or not so) on Earth. This update is not as big as the last one in terms of game-changing content, I mostly did the groundwork in preparation for v0.24 which will be huge feature and content wise.

Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year!
